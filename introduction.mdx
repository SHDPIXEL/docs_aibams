## What is AIBAMS?

**AIBAMS** (Autonomous AI Infrastructure for Business Management Systems) is a unified AI-powered operating environment designed to simplify how modern businesses work, grow, and scale.

At its core, AIBAMS provides a **single intelligent layer** that unifies communication, storage, AI agents, automation, and web presence — replacing the need for dozens of disconnected tools.
