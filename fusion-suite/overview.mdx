## What is Fusion Suite?

**Fusion Suite** is AIBAMS's flagship integrated product — a collection of AI-powered business applications that run on a shared intelligent infrastructure called FusionOS.

The core promise of Fusion Suite: **Run your entire business on one intelligent layer.**

Instead of subscribing to separate tools for email, website, storage, and AI, Fusion Suite delivers all of these — tightly integrated, context-aware, and managed from a single dashboard.

## Integration Highlights

### Shared Context & Memory

All Fusion Suite apps share a **unified memory layer**. This means:

- Your AI assistant knows your email threads without you copy-pasting
- Files you upload are immediately available for AI analysis
- Your website contact forms route directly into your MailX inbox
- Customer context carries across every touchpoint

### Single Sign-On

One login. One workspace. All apps.

No managing separate passwords or accounts for each tool. FusionOS handles authentication centrally.

### Unified Billing

One subscription covers the entire suite — not per-app pricing. As new applications are added, they're automatically included in your plan.
